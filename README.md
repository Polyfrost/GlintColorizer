# GlintColorizer

[![Modrinth downloads](https://img.shields.io/modrinth/dt/SevsTfWN?style=flat&logo=modrinth&label=Modrinth&color=1bd96a)](https://modrinth.com/mod/glintcolorizer)

GlintColorizer recolors the enchantment glint. You can change its speed, rotation and size too.

- Separate settings for held items, GUI items, dropped items, item frames and armor
- Color the two layers of the glint individually
- Remove the glint from armor entirely
- Apply one color (or the 1.7 glint color) everywhere with one click
- You can use `/glintcolorizer` to open your settings

### Shiny Pots

Shiny Pots gives potions in your inventory a different look.

- Stretch the glint over the whole slot
- Show the glint behind the bottle instead of on it
- Color the glint to match the potion
- Or give potions a custom glint of their own

## Compatibility

GlintColorizer works with Argentum's GUI rendering (Shiny Pots can cost some FPS there in potion-heavy inventories) and with the 1.7 and 1.15 glint styles from OverflowAnimations.

## Supported versions

- Minecraft 1.8.9 on Ornithe.

GlintColorizer needs [OneConfig](https://modrinth.com/mod/oneconfig) and Fabric Language Kotlin.
